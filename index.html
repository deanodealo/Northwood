— What's On

Activities For Everyone

Wednesday Night Football

Wednesday Night Football is our elite indoor coaching programme focused on technical development, game intelligence, and confidence on the ball. Delivered in partnership with professional coaches, sessions are structured, high-energy, and suitable for players of all abilities. A great pathway for those looking to improve and enjoy the game.

Click here to book your session →

Funetics

Funetics is a fun, engaging multi-skills session designed to build children's confidence, coordination, and love for physical activity. Through games and structured activities, kids develop fundamental movement skills in a positive, supportive environment. Perfect for younger children starting their sporting journey.
