— How Can We Help?

Two Ways to Book

For schools

School Session Hire

Regular weekly lessons on our athletics track, sports hall or 3G pitch, with a flat school rate.

  • £30 per hour flat rate — Athletics Track, Sports Hall (1/3) or 3G Pitch (1/3)
  • Invoiced termly in advance, payment due within 14 days
  • Sessions missed due to INSET days, half term or bank holidays aren't charged
  • A short site induction is arranged before your first session
Start School Booking

For events

Host an Event

Sports days, tournaments, college fixtures, company and community events — using one space or the whole venue.

  • 10% non-refundable deposit confirms your date
  • Balance due no later than 7 days before the event
  • Vendors on site: £50 flat fee per vendor
  • Large spectated events: 1 additional member of staff per 150 spectators
  • A pre-event call or site meeting is required for every event
Start Event Booking

3Pricing & Site Induction
School Sports Hire: £30.00 per hour (flat). Confirmed regular slots are invoiced termly, in advance, based on the number of sessions booked for that term. Payment is due within 14 days of invoice. Sessions missed due to school closure (INSET days, half term, bank holidays) are not charged.

Site induction is required before the first session. A short induction covering site access, changing rooms, safeguarding contacts and emergency procedures will be arranged with the school beforehand.
