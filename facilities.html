— 3G Football

3G Football Pitches

Our floodlit 3G pitches provide a consistent, high-quality playing surface suitable for everything from casual five-a-side to full eleven-a-side fixtures. Whether you're booking a one-off game or running a season-long league, the pitches are ready when you are.

  • Floodlit for evening bookings
  • FA-standard playing surface
  • Five-a-side through to full-size setups
  • Ideal for leagues, training and tournaments
  • On-site changing rooms

— Gym

Gym

Our fully equipped gym gives you everything you need to train your way, in a friendly, welcoming space built around the community — not intimidating rows of machines. Whether you're just starting out or training for competition, there's flexible access to fit around your routine.

  • Free weights and resistance machines
  • Dedicated cardio suite & group classes
  • Flexible membership options
  • Friendly, inclusive environment
  • Open extended hours, seven days a week

— Sports Hall

Sports Hall

Our versatile indoor hall is built to flex around whatever you need — five-a-side football, netball, badminton, or a community programme of your own. Bookable by the hour, it's a reliable space for regular sessions or one-off events.

  • Large multi-sport indoor hall
  • Facilities for Five-a-Side, Netball and Badminton
  • Also, Basketball, Table Tennis and Futsal
  • Bookable by the hour
  • Ideal for community programmes and events

— Track

Athletics Track

Staffordshire's only certified track and field facility, our national-standard 400m track is built for serious training and competition, while staying open and welcoming to newcomers finding their feet. Clubs, schools and individual members all train here side by side.

  • National-standard 400m track
  • Certified for competition use
  • Open to clubs, schools and individuals
  • Floodlit for evening training
  • Full field event facilities on site
