Strength & conditioning

Functional Fitness Room

Our converted squash court, built for Hyrox-style and functional training. Push the sled down the turf lane, hit the rowers, swing kettlebells and work through circuits that build strength you can use.

  • Sled track
  • Kettlebells
  • Wall balls
  • Sandbags
  • Plyo boxes
  • Rowing machines
  • SkiErg
  • Battle ropes
  • Hex bars
Machine training

Resistance & Weights Room

Guided resistance machines that let you train every major muscle group safely. Ideal if you're new to the gym, coming back from a break, or adding volume to your programme.

  • Resistance machines
  • Cable stations
  • Lat pulldown
  • Leg extension & curl
  • Dumbbells
  • Stability balls
Endurance

Cardio Section

Warm up, build your engine or settle into a steady session at your own pace. Pair it with the track outside for interval work you won't get in a high-street gym.

  • Treadmills
  • Cross-trainers
  • Exercise bikes
  • Views over the track
Lifting

Free Weights Room

Dumbbells, barbells and room to lift. Squat, press and pull with the space to progress properly, away from the machines.

  • Dumbbells up to 40kg
  • Fixed barbells
  • Benches
  • Mats
Train together

Fitness Classes

Coach-led group sessions for every fitness level. Train alongside others, stay motivated and learn new skills with someone guiding you through every round.
