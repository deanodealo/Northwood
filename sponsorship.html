— Questions

Sponsorship FAQs

How big are the boards?

Each board is [size, e.g. 2.4m × 0.6m], printed on [material] and fixed along the track or 3G pitch-side barrier.

Do I need to design my own board?

Send us your logo and brand colours. Printing is included in every package, and we'll prepare the artwork for you at no extra cost.

How does the 2-year discount work?

Commit for two years and you get 10% off. A Supporter package comes to £720 over two years instead of £800, a Community Partner package £1,350 instead of £1,500, and a Premier Partner package £3,240 instead of £3,600.

What happens when my sponsorship ends?

You get first refusal to keep your board positions when you renew, so nobody else can take your spot while you decide.

How do the free gym and track passes work?

Passes are for your employees and are valid for 12 months from the start of your sponsorship. Each pass can be used for [one gym or track session].
