— What Is Funetics?

Fun First. Skills for Life.

Created by England Athletics, funetics is a fun and inclusive kids athletics programme that helps 4 to 11-year-old children across the country learn, develop and practise running, jumping and throwing all year round, for a healthy, confident future.

Helping children have fun while getting active, funetics provides a safe, fun and nurturing environment for children to develop fundamental movement skills and the confidence to support them in different sports in the future.

England Athletics' vision

For every primary school aged child in the country to be able to access funetics sessions, learning the fundamental movement skills of running, throwing and jumping through a trackable, physical literacy-based course.

— The Fundamentals

Run. Jump. Throw.

Every session is built around the three skills at the heart of athletics, taught through games and challenges children actually want to do.

Run

Speed, agility and balance through relays, races and chase games.

Jump

Hops, bounds and leaps that build power, coordination and landing control.

Throw

Safe, kid-sized throwing kit to learn technique and hand-eye coordination.

— Parents' Questions

Good to Know

Does my child need any athletics experience?

Not at all. Funetics is designed for complete beginners as well as children who already love sport.

Which session should we choose?

Tuesdays are fun, games-based sessions for all ages, a great place to start. Saturdays are more technical and split by age (4–7 and 8+), so they suit children who want to build on what they've learned. Plenty of children do both.

What should they wear and bring?
